Questions people ask

Is Karo Split free?

Yes. You can create as many trips and expenses as you like without paying anything.

Do my friends need to sign up?

No. Only the person keeping track needs an account. Friends are just names in the trip, and you can share the PDF report with them at the end.

Where is my data saved?

Your trips are saved in the browser on your device. Clearing your browser data removes them, so export a PDF or Excel copy before you do.

Can someone else see my trips?

On a shared device, each account is protected by a 4-digit PIN, so others can’t open your trips. The PIN is meant to keep people apart on one device, so don’t reuse a PIN that protects something important.

Can I split an expense unequally?

Yes. Choose “Exact amounts” and type what each person owes, and Karo Split checks that the shares add up to the total.

Which currencies are supported?

Indian rupee, US dollar, euro, pound, UAE dirham, Thai baht, Singapore dollar, Japanese yen, Australian dollar and Canadian dollar. Each trip uses one currency.
